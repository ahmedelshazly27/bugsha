Mark concepts

All monochrome, all drawn on the same 48-grid, all shown at 72px and again at 28 / 20 / 16px so you can judge them at favicon and tab-bar size. Concepts 1–4 are the bundle read literally; 5–8 abstract the wrap or the knot; 9–12 push toward monogram, seal and pickup-window ideas.

Wordmark pairings

Each row sets Bugsha and بقشة at matched optical weight. The Arabic is not a fallback — the pairing is chosen so both scripts share the same rhythm and stroke contrast.
