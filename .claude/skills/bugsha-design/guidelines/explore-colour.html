Two colours, three at the absolute most — the way Too Good To Go (deep teal + warm off-white), Talabat, Deliveroo and most F&B brands actually work: one surface, one ink, and at most one accent. Teal and eco-green are off the table — they read as a category clone and, in Kuwait, as NGO rather than F&B. The swatch strip under each name is the entire palette: nothing else is allowed in, and status (fresh, time, urgent, error) is carried by tint depth, weight and icon — never by a new hue.
