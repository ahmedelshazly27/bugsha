The nightly write-off

You already paid for the food you throw away at closing.

اللي ترميه بالليل، أنت دافع ثمنه من قبل.

CostIngredients, labour and energy are already spent. Every discarded tray is margin you funded twice.
WasteDisposal is a line item, not a free action — bags, bins, collection, and the staff minutes at close.
SilenceDiscounting in-store trains regulars to wait for a markdown. A separate channel doesn't.

Bugsha is a second, quiet channel that opens only at the end of your day, priced by you, sold to people who are not your full-price customers.

Three actions. Sixty seconds.

No new hardware. No integration with your POS. It runs on the phone already in your manager's pocket.
